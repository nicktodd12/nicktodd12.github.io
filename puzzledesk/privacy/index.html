PuzzleDesk

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 16, 2026

Plain-language summary

Puzzle gameplay and personal statistics stay in local app storage. PuzzleDesk has no account system and no first-party analytics or crash-reporting service. Google services handle Android ads, consent, purchases, and optional Play review flows.

1. Scope

This policy explains how PuzzleDesk, developed by Off Hours Studio, handles information in the app. Platform services such as Google Play and Google Mobile Ads also process information under their own policies.

2. Information stored locally

PuzzleDesk is designed as a local-first app. It stores information needed to continue games and show personal progress, including:

  • Current and original puzzle boards, generated puzzle identity and configuration, player entries, elapsed active time, hints, checks, completion state, and undo or redo history.
  • Completion records such as puzzle type, difficulty, completion date, active play duration, hint and check counts, and whether developer assistance was used.
  • Preferences such as appearance, navigation style, last-played puzzle, onboarding state, review-reminder state, and advertising frequency history.
  • On Android, simple cached ownership flags for permanent Remove Ads and Supporter purchases.

This information is stored in private app preferences on Android, platform preferences on iOS, or local user preferences on desktop. PuzzleDesk does not upload this gameplay or statistics information to Off Hours Studio.

Backups

Android backup is enabled. Depending on your device and Google backup settings, Android may back up and restore eligible local app data through the operating system. That processing is controlled by Android and your Google account settings, not by an Off Hours Studio server.

3. Advertising and consent

PuzzleDesk is prepared to use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) in an upcoming Android production release. When advertising is enabled, PuzzleDesk may display banner and interstitial ads. The app requests ads only when UMP reports that ads may be requested, and verified Remove Ads or Supporter ownership suppresses advertising.

According to Google, the Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • App and advertisement interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.
  • Device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and applicable device-related identifiers.

Google states that this information may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention and is encrypted in transit. The exact ads and data uses can depend on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google account settings.

UMP checks consent information when the app starts, presents a consent form when required, and makes a privacy-options entry point available in Settings when Google indicates that one is required.

Learn more from Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure, Google's UMP documentation, and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Google Play purchases

Android purchases use Google Play Billing. Google Play processes payment details, account information, transaction history, and payment authorization. PuzzleDesk does not receive your full payment-card details.

The app receives product information, localized prices, purchase state, product identifiers, and purchase tokens needed to complete, acknowledge, consume, or restore transactions. Purchase tokens are used during billing operations; PuzzleDesk does not intentionally persist purchase tokens, order IDs, or Google account details. It stores only local confirmed-ownership flags for permanent products so an existing entitlement is not lost during a temporary connection failure.

Permanent ownership is reconciled with Google Play at startup, when the app returns to the foreground, and when you choose Restore Purchases. Extra Support is a repeatable contribution and is consumed through Google Play; it does not create a permanent entitlement.

Google's handling of purchase information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play terms.

5. Google Play reviews

PuzzleDesk may ask Google Play to present its standard in-app review interface when that feature is enabled. PuzzleDesk cannot determine whether you submitted a rating and does not receive or store the text of a review. Reviews submitted through Google Play are handled by Google under its policies.

6. Analytics, crash reporting, and diagnostics

PuzzleDesk does not currently include a first-party or third-party analytics SDK, and it does not include a third-party crash-reporting SDK such as Firebase Crashlytics or Sentry.

PuzzleDesk does not operate a diagnostic collection service or transmit its own diagnostic logs to Off Hours Studio.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may independently collect its own diagnostic and interaction information as described in the advertising section above.

7. Accounts, permissions, and sensitive information

PuzzleDesk does not require or provide a PuzzleDesk account. The app does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location for gameplay. General location may be inferred by Google from an IP address when Google advertising services operate, as described above.

8. Retention and your choices

  • Local gameplay, preferences, and statistics remain until changed, cleared through system app settings, or removed by uninstalling the app, subject to operating-system backup and restore.
  • Where required, you can revisit Google privacy choices through PuzzleDesk Settings. You can also control or reset the Android advertising ID and ad personalization through your device or Google settings.
  • Google Play retains purchase records according to Google's policies. You can manage purchases through Google Play.
  • Because PuzzleDesk has no Off Hours Studio account or gameplay-data server, Off Hours Studio does not hold a server-side gameplay profile to delete.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when PuzzleDesk's features, SDKs, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest revision.

10. Contact
