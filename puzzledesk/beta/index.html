A little play.
A big help.

Help shape a calmer puzzle break. PuzzleDesk brings three endlessly replayable games together, with offline play, helpful hints, and progress saved on your device.

Join the beta Google Group

Three ways to take a break

01 · Queens

Find the perfect spot

Place one queen in every row, column, and colored region. Keep queens from touching.

02 · Balance

Bring it into balance

Balance Suns and Moons across the grid, using X and = clues to connect neighboring cells.

03 · Memory Match

Make a connection

Turn over cards and find the matching pairs. A relaxed test of recall, at your own pace.

Getting started

Your path to the beta

Use the same Google account for the Group and Google Play on your Android device.

  1. 1
    Available now

    Join the Google Group

    Open the PuzzleDesk beta Group, sign in to your Google account, and choose Join group. Membership makes that account eligible for the closed test once it is published.

    Join the beta Group

    Joining the Group is the first step; it does not install the app or opt you into Google Play testing.

  2. 2
    Coming soon

    Opt into Google Play testing

    After the closed test is published, return here for the Google Play opt-in link. Open it with the same account you used to join the Group and accept the testing invitation.

    Google Play opt-in link: not available yet.

  3. 3
    Coming soon

    Install, play, and share feedback

    Once you have opted in, install PuzzleDesk from Google Play on your Android device. Try the games and tell us what felt good, what was confusing, or what needs fixing.

    Google Play install link: not available yet.
