A little play.
A big help.

Help shape a calmer puzzle break. PuzzleDesk brings three endlessly replayable games together, with offline play, helpful hints, and progress saved on your device.

Three ways to take a break

01 · Queens

Find the perfect spot

Place one queen in every row, column, and colored region. Keep queens from touching.

02 · Balance

Bring it into balance

Balance Suns and Moons across the grid, using X and = clues to connect neighboring cells.

03 · Memory Match

Make a connection

Turn over cards and find the matching pairs. A relaxed test of recall, at your own pace.

Getting started

Join in three steps

Use the same Google account to join the Google Group, become a Google Play tester, and install PuzzleDesk on your Android device.

  1. 1
    Available now

    Join the beta Google Group

    Open the PuzzleDesk beta Group, sign in to the Google account you will use for testing, and choose Join group. Membership makes that account eligible for the closed test.

    Join the beta Group

    Joining the Group is the first step; it does not install the app or opt you into Google Play testing.

  2. 2
    Available now

    Become a tester

    After joining the Group, open the Google Play testing page with the same Google account and select Become a tester.

    Become a tester
  3. 3
    Available now

    Install PuzzleDesk on Android

    Once you have joined the Group and become a tester, install PuzzleDesk from Google Play on your Android device using that same Google account. Then try the games and share your feedback.

    Install PuzzleDesk

    Complete steps 1 and 2 first. The installation link may not work until you have joined the Group and opted into testing. Check that Google Play is signed in to the same account.
