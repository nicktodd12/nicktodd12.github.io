A little play.
A big help.

Help shape a calmer puzzle break. PuzzleDesk brings three endlessly replayable games together, with offline play, helpful hints, and progress saved on your device.

Getting started

Join in three steps

Use the same Google account to join the Google Group, become a Google Play tester, and install PuzzleDesk on your Android device.

  1. 1
    Available now

    Join the beta group

    Open PuzzleDesk Beta in Google Groups and select Join group.

    Join the beta group

    Can't view group conversations? Google Groups may show “You don't have access to this content” even after joining. This doesn't necessarily mean membership failed. Check Google Groups → My groups to confirm you're a member of PuzzleDesk Beta. You don't need to read group messages to participate in testing.

  2. 2
    Available now

    Become a Google Play tester

    Open the Google Play testing page and select Become a tester to opt in.

    Become a Google Play tester
  3. 3
    Available now

    Install PuzzleDesk on Android

    Download PuzzleDesk from Google Play on your Android device.

    Install PuzzleDesk

    Complete steps 1 and 2 first. The installation link may show the app as unavailable until you have joined the group and become a tester.

Three ways to take a break

01 · Queens

Find the perfect spot

Place one queen in every row, column, and colored region. Keep queens from touching.

02 · Balance

Bring it into balance

Balance Suns and Moons across the grid, using X and = clues to connect neighboring cells.

03 · Memory Match

Make a connection

Turn over cards and find the matching pairs. A relaxed test of recall, at your own pace.
